These were taken on a single afternoon in mid-September, across two beaches.

The first two are Woljeongri. The water close in is bright turquoise; a little further out it turns suddenly, deeply blue, as though someone had drawn a line across the sea with a ruler. Black volcanic boulders lie along the sand, bleached pale by the sun.

The last two are Hamdeok, where the sea is far shallower — you can walk a long way out and still be only waist-deep. As the sun leaned west, drums started up from the stage on the shore: a performance nobody had announced.
